## App info

I wanted to build a shows tracker that keeps tracks of the shows I have watched, am currently watching and have a way to list and review them.

Serialized sort of does the same thing but I am someone who loses track of shows becuase I stopped watching them them mid-way, lost a streaming service. I also want a way to see how much of the show I have watched (percent-wise) and how many hours are left.

(This is a work in progress project I am doing for fun)
